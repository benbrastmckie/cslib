## Propositional — 3 sorries, one root cause, and a routing problem

### The dependency structure

All three sorries sit in the tableau completeness layer:

| Site | Declaration | Nature |
|------|-------------|--------|
| `Tableau/Intuitionistic/Scheme.lean:8034` | `openBranch_countermodel` | The whole `∃ edges` existential — **OPEN, not refuted** |
| `Tableau/Intuitionistic/Completeness.lean:170` | `intuitionisticTableau_complete` (DP-3) | Delegates to the above |
| `Tableau/Minimal/Completeness.lean:166` | `minimalTableau_complete` (DP-4) | Delegates, **plus** a genuinely separate obligation |

DP-3 is a pure delegation: the in-source note at `Completeness.lean:150-169` is explicit that
discharging it with `exact h Nat (intExtractValuation _b) _huc 0` would type-check but "would only
launder that still-open conjunct through this file." DP-4 additionally needs upward-closure of
`minBranchBotForces b` at the `bot` shape, which task 605 documents as a distinct obligation that
holds at the `[(1,0)]` witness but is **not established in general**.

So: **two of the three sorries fall the moment the existential is proved; the third (DP-4) needs
one extra lemma.**

### The existential is genuinely open, and five routes are already dead

`Scheme.lean`'s docstring records a frame-adequacy table with machine-checked verdicts. Refuted or
excluded: `rawEdges`; pruning at blocked worlds; pruning at strictly-blocked worlds; the greatest
`IFimpAccess`-supported fixpoint; the maximal atom-inclusion frame `⊑`. The residual obligation is
stated precisely: *a frame carrying `IFimpAccess` and positive persistence simultaneously, which
the current calculus does not produce.*

This is not a small residual. `Completeness.lean:159-161` states the remaining work "is equivalent
to proving the tableau procedure complete: genuine open work."

### The quality of the record here is exceptional

Worth saying plainly: the in-source annotations in `Scheme.lean` and both `Completeness.lean`
files are among the most honest formalization records I have reviewed. They distinguish
*refuted* from *excluded* from *open*, name the machine witnesses (`CslibTests/`), record why
earlier `refine` attempts were reverted (they put a refuted statement behind a `sorry`), and
explicitly forbid the type-checking-but-laundering discharge. Nothing here is disguised as
progress. That discipline is why this review can be confident about scope.

  `modalApplyOneD` already satisfies `boxPosNotExpanding`; the sole failing field was
  `outputsSubsetUniverse`, for a universe reason. It then built `DDriver.lean` — **1,327 lines**,
  registered in `Cslib.lean`, carrying the complete twelve-field witness `modalApplyOneD_specAt`,

  completion record that this **"unblock[s] D/DB/D4/D5/D45"** — at a cost of ~54 insertions / 30
  deletions, versus the 755-line estimate its own description carried.

So D's expensive part is already spent. What is missing is the final wiring that every covered

| Covered corner (e.g. T) | D |
|--------------------------|---|
| `tValid` | **absent** |
| `modalTableauT_sound` | **absent** |
| `modalTableauT_complete` | **absent** |

**Recommended fix**: (a) Create a task to wire `dValid` / `modalTableauD_sound` /

598 and 601 landed. (c) Decide explicitly whether the remaining 6 corners are in scope at all —
at Tier C prices (~13,500 lines each for K4/D4) they may be a deliberate permanent exclusion
rather than a backlog.

## Answering the three questions

### What remains

**Propositional** — three bare sorries, all in tableau completeness, all downstream of one open
existential, plus one independent lemma at DP-4. Behind them sit four non-proof tasks: 375 (fold
tableau edges into the TFAE), 400 (unbundle connective typeclasses / upstream PR #607), 497 (the
`imp`→`impl` rename), 409 (optional, blocked on an abandoned redesign).

**Modal** — no proof debt at all. Seven unbuilt decidability corners (one of which, D, is nearly
finished and unowned), the constructive CS5 line (open mathematics), one completeness gap (534,
pure-K5 Euclidean), and three hygiene tasks (588, 589, 590, 600).

### How it divides up

Four groups, in descending confidence that they can actually be finished:

1. **Cheap and owned** — the hygiene tail: L1's one-command ratchet, 588, 590, and the metadata
   repairs in M1–M3. Hours.
2. **Cheap and unowned** — wiring the D corner (C1). The hard 1,327 lines are built; this is the
   single best value-per-effort item in either logic, and nothing is scheduled to do it.
3. **Hard but bounded** — the propositional completeness cluster (609 → 605 → 606). Bounded
   because the target is precisely stated and `truthLemma` is already sorry-free and parametric;
   hard because five constructions are dead and the remaining obligation is a calculus-level
   change in `Expansion.lean`. This is the only work that turns the build green.
4. **Open-ended** — constructive CS5 (554 → 537, 551), and the Tier B/C decidability corners at
   ~3,600–13,500 lines each. These are scope decisions, not schedule items.

### How substantial it is

Empirically calibrated from this repo's own history:

- Routine subtasks of the propositional cluster (601, 602, 603, 604) each landed in 4–8 commits
  within a single day.
- One genuine decidability corner (S4, task 511) took **51 commits over 3.5 weeks**.
- Task 598 delivered a 1,327-line driver in a single day; task 601 discharged a 755-line estimate
  in ~54 insertions.

So: group 1 is hours. Group 2 is plausibly days, given the pattern is established at seven other
corners. Group 3 is the real question mark — it is one open mathematical obstruction, and the
honest answer is that it could fall in a week or resist indefinitely; the repo's own annotations
say as much. Group 4, taken in full, is comparable in size to the existing Modal tree and should
be scoped out rather than scheduled.

**Bottom line**: getting the build green and both logics "done modulo declared exclusions" is
dominated by *one* open mathematical question (the `openBranch_countermodel` existential) plus
*one* unowned afternoon of wiring (the D corner). Everything else is either hygiene or an explicit
scope decision. The largest risk is not difficulty — it is that both of those items are currently
mis-routed in the task graph (H1) or invisible in the source documentation (C1).
