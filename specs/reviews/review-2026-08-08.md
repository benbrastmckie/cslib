## Critical Issues

### C1. Task 592's founding premise is false — the refutation evidence exists and is inspectable

**File**: `specs/state.json` (task 592); `specs/reviews/review-2026-08-07.md` C3

**Description**: Task 592 asserts that `scratch/BetaSplitRefutation.lean`,
`scratch/HvalidShapeRefutation.lean` and `scratch/PersistPrototype.lean` "DO NOT EXIST in the
repository", and concludes that three permanent-deferral decisions "rest on evidence nobody can
open". All three files are present at HEAD:

```
specs/archive/430_prove_atom_persistence_upward_closure_for_intexpan/scratch/
  BetaSplitRefutation.lean      (396 lines; `phiRef1` at :235, `report` at :213)
  HvalidShapeRefutation.lean    (84 lines; `phiK_valid : IValid phiK` at :65,
                                 `valuation_not_upward_closed` at :57)
  PersistPrototype.lean
```

The `scratch/…` prefix in the citations is **task-directory-relative, not repository-root
relative**. `Scheme.lean:3474` already writes the full form
(`specs/430_.../scratch/PersistPrototype.lean`), which confirms the convention. The review that
created 592 resolved the prefix against the repository root, found no `scratch/` there, and
inferred loss.

**Impact**: Two-fold, in opposite directions. The good news is that no evidence was lost and no
work was permanently abandoned in error — the three deferral decisions are auditable today. The
bad news is that task 592 is scoped around a recovery-or-downgrade decision that does not need to
be made, and its stated fallback ("DOWNGRADE the annotations … to `[UNVERIFIED]`, evidence lost")
would actively corrupt correct records. 591 names 592 as its prerequisite specifically because
evidence was thought unavailable; that gate is weaker than believed.

**Recommended fix**: Rewrite 592's scope to what the defect actually is — 14 stale citation
paths across 4 files (`Minimal/Completeness.lean` ×2, `Intuitionistic/Completeness.lean` ×4,
`Intuitionistic/Expansion.lean` ×1, `Intuitionistic/Scheme.lean` ×7) — plus promotion of
`phiRef1` and the `hvalid`-shape counterexample into `CslibTests/`, following the established
`CslibTests/AncestorRedirectRefutation.lean` precedent. Delete the "irrecoverable" branch.
