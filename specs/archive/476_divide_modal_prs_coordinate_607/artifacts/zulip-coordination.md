No worries about the volume, and no rush — enjoy the time off. I'm in SF so the 3:30am slot is rough on my end, but I'd be glad to jump on a call once you're back on the 23rd.

To shrink the pile a bit before then: I'd like to propose one change to the `Proposition` type in [#607](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pull/607). Right now `box` is derived from `diamond` (`□φ := ¬◇¬φ`). Could we make both `box` and `diamond` primitive constructors instead? The reason isn't cosmetic: in the intuitionistic and constructive modal systems (IK, CK) I want to build on this later, `□` and `◇` are not interdefinable (Simpson's 1994 thesis on intuitionistic modal logic; Wijesekera, "Constructive Modal Logics I", 1990). Keeping both primitive now means the same datatype carries into those systems unchanged, and it also lets Necessitation and K be stated directly on primitive constructors.

While we're in that type, I'd also suggest making `bot`, `imp`, `and`, and `or` native constructors, for a seven-constructor `Proposition` `{atom, bot, imp, and, or, box, diamond}` (with `¬`/`⊤` derived). That part is more consistency than necessity — it mirrors the propositional `Proposition` and gives one clean decomposition rule per connective for the semantic/tableau layer. But if you'd rather keep [#607](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pull/607) minimal, the independent `box`/`diamond` is the piece I actually need; the rest can wait. Either way I'm happy to open a small PR on top of [#607](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pull/607) rather than push into yours.

On the back of that, I've started reworking [#662](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pull/662) into a foundational semantic layer for modal logic on these primitives: the satisfaction relation, a denotational semantics, the duality theorem `◇φ ↔ ¬□¬φ` (now a proved theorem rather than a definition), one decomposition lemma per connective, and K-axiom validity. It's sorry-free and CI-green, but I've kept it as a local commit rather than pushing, since it depends on the primitive question above — happy to push it for review once we settle the primitive set, and to split it if it's too much for one PR.

Separately, there's still the propositional design question from before I'd value your call on: [#648](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pull/648) makes `⊥` a primitive constructor (five primitives `{atom, bot, imp, and, or}`, `¬A := A → ⊥`), whereas [#607](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pull/607) keeps four with `⊥` as `atom ⊥` under `[Bot Atom]`. Thomas has reviewed and approved [#648](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pull/648) and I've addressed his inline comments; Ching-Tsun earlier favoured a primitive `⊥` (Matthew agreed) — so there's support for the five-primitive base, but since both touch the same type we'd need to pick one to standardise on.
