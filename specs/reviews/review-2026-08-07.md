   `openBranch_countermodel` disposition, restate the three completeness theorems, close

   abandonment decisions currently rest on files nobody can open.

   re-discoveries of findings already recorded in `specs/ROADMAP-alignment-audit.md`. A one-time
   cleanup will regress again without a validation gate; there is no dependency-integrity check
   in `.claude/scripts/` today, and `generate-task-order.sh` silently treats archived-task edges
   as satisfied, which is what makes the staleness invisible.

4. **Decide the tableau driver abstraction before expanding the cube.** Three termination regimes,
   one abstraction covering one of them, eight corners queued.
