No worries at all, and sorry about the volume. I'd be glad to join a CSLib meeting once you're back on the 23rd, though I'm in SF so 3:30am meeting time might be tricky for me, but happy to jump on a call.

To shrink the pile a little before then: I'd like to suggest a small change to your [#607](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pull/607) `Proposition` type before I stack [#662](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pull/662) on it, since it makes the modal semantic layer I've since built out considerably cleaner. Rather than defining `box` in terms of `diamond` (`□φ := ¬◇¬φ`), could we make both `box` and `diamond` primitive constructors, alongside a primitive `bot`/`imp`/`and`/`or` — i.e. a fully-native seven-constructor `Proposition`: `{atom, bot, imp, and, or, box, diamond}`? Two reasons: (1) with every connective primitive, the satisfaction/decomposition lemma for each one is `Iff.rfl` rather than an unfold-through-a-definition proof, which matters once you're doing structural induction over the whole type (tableau rules, truth lemma, canonical model); (2) `□`/`◇` genuinely need to be independent for the intuitionistic and minimal modal systems (IK, CK) down the line, where neither is definable from the other. Necessitation and K still only ever touch `□`, so this doesn't add any proof-theoretic weight. I've written up the fuller case as a PR comment (not posted yet, pending your OK) and I'm glad to walk through it live on the 23rd if that's easier than text.

On the back of that, I've put together a substantial rework of [#662](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pull/662) — not just the small box-alongside-diamond delta from before, but a full foundational semantic layer for modal logic built on the native primitives: the satisfaction relation, a denotational semantics, the duality theorem `◇φ ↔ ¬□¬φ` (now proved semantically rather than holding by definition), one decomposition lemma per connective, and the K-axiom validity theorem. It's sorry-free and CI-verified, but it's currently sitting as a local commit rather than pushed, since it depends on [#607](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pull/607) adopting the native primitives first — happy to push and open it for review as soon as we've agreed on the primitive set above.

There's also still the propositional design question from before that I'd value your view on: **[#648](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pull/648) reworks `Logics/Propositional` to make `⊥` a primitive constructor** (a five-primitive `{atom, bot, imp, and, or}` with `¬A := A → ⊥`), whereas [#607](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pull/607)'s `Proposition` keeps four primitives with `⊥` as `atom ⊥` under `[Bot Atom]`. Thomas has already reviewed and approved [#648](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pull/648) (I've since addressed his inline comments), and Chris earlier said he liked adding `⊥` as a primitive — so there's support for the five-primitive base, but since both PRs touch the same `Proposition` type they can't simply layer and we'd need to pick one to standardise on.
