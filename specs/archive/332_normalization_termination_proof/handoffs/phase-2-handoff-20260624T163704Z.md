# Phase 2 Handoff: Measure Decrease Lemmas

## Status: PARTIAL

Phase 2 aimed to prove 3 measure decrease lemmas. The conjunction beta-redex cases
(2 of 8 reduceRoot patterns) are proved. The remaining 6 cases are blocked on
two fundamental issues.

## What Was Completed

1. **Proved `reduceRoot_andE_maxFormulas_lt`**: For `andE1 _ (andI _ D1 D2)` -> `D1`,
   the Dershowitz-Manna multiset ordering strictly decreases. Witness: `X = D1.mf`,
   `Y = empty`, `Z = {conclusionComplexity} + D2.mf`.

2. **Proved `reduceRoot_andE2_maxFormulas_lt`**: Symmetric case for `andE2`.

3. **Proved cases h_2 and h_3** within `reduceRoot_decreases_normMeasure`: conjunction
   projection cases use `Prod.Lex.left` with the DM ordering decrease.

4. **Restructured theorem statements**: Replaced the original 3 sorry theorems with
   better-documented versions. `reduceRoot_beta_maxFormulas_lt` was split into two
   standalone helper lemmas for the conjunction cases.

## What Is Blocked

### Blocker 1: Substitution Beta-Redex Cases (h_1, h_4, h_5)

Cases involving `subsOne`:
- `impE (impI _ D) E` -> `D.subsOne E`
- `orE _ (orI1 _ D) DA _` -> `DA.subsOne D`
- `orE _ (orI2 _ D) _ DB` -> `DB.subsOne D`

**Why blocked**: `subsOne` is defined via `subs`, which uses tactic blocks (`by grind`,
`weakCtx` rewrites) at `ass`, `orE`, and `impI` branches. This makes structural induction
on the output technically challenging. The mathematical argument is sound (new redexes can
only involve proper subformulas of the cut formula) but the formalization path through `subs`
is unclear.

**What is needed**: Either:
(a) Prove `subsOne_maximalFormulas_complexity_bound` by induction on `body`, showing that
    `subs` preserves maximal formula bounds. This requires ~100 lines of case analysis.
(b) Refactor `subsOne` to use a pattern-matching definition instead of going through `subs`,
    making structural properties easier to prove.
(c) Use a different termination argument that avoids reasoning about `subsOne` output.

### Blocker 2: Commuting Conversion Cases (h_6, h_7, h_8)

Cases pushing elimination inside `orE`:
- `andE1 G (orE G D DA DB)` -> `orE G D (andE1 _ DA) (andE1 _ DB)`
- `andE2 G (orE _ D DA DB)` -> similar
- `impE (orE G D DA DB) E` -> similar

**Why blocked**: Commuting conversions do NOT generally decrease `maximalFormulas`.
If `DA` or `DB` is `andI` (or `impI`, `orI1`, `orI2`), pushing `andE1` inside creates
a NEW beta-redex that wasn't there before. The standard Prawitz argument requires subterms
to be already normalized (SN) before doing commuting conversions. This means:

1. The `h_allSubsSN` hypothesis in `reduceRoot_decreases_normMeasure` must be strengthened
   from `True` to an actual SN hypothesis on immediate subterms of `d`.
2. For commuting cases, one must show `maximalFormulas` is EQUAL (not just ≤) when
   subterms are SN (because SN subterms cannot be introduction forms at redex positions).
3. Then use `Prod.Lex.right` with `commutingSum` strictly decreasing.

**What is needed**: Strengthen `h_allSubsSN` to provide actual SN information about
subterms, then prove:
- When `DA`, `DB` are SN, `andE1 _ DA` creates no new maximal formulas (because `DA`
  can't be `andI` -- SN rules that out since it would create a redex).
- Similarly for the other commuting patterns.
- `commutingSum` strictly decreases (the original site contributes `nodeCount(orE)`
  which goes away).
