## High Priority Issues

### H1. The canonical-model truth lemma is proved three times; the generic one lives in a leaf file
**Files**: `Cslib/Logics/Modal/Metalogic/Systems/K/Completeness.lean:163` (`k_truth_lemma`), `Metalogic/Completeness.lean:274` (`truth_lemma`), `Systems/D/Completeness.lean:241` (`d_truth_lemma`)
**Description**: `k_truth_lemma` is fully generic — its `.box` case needs only EFQ + K (present in `kCore`, hence in all 15 axiom predicates). `truth_lemma` demands a semantically unnecessary `h_T`; `d_truth_lemma` an unnecessary `h_D`. ~545 duplicated lines; system routing is split three ways (T/S4/TB/S5 → `truth_lemma`; D/D4/D5/D45/DB → `d_truth_lemma`; rest → `k_truth_lemma`). Compounding this, the shared machinery (`k_derive_box_from_inconsistency`, `k_mcs_box_witness`, `d_canonical_serial`) lives in the K/D leaf files, which are 2-3× the size of their 179-214-line siblings — load-bearing infrastructure masquerading as instances.
**Impact**: The most general proof is the least discoverable; every new system chooses among three redundant routes; K/D imports drag in shared machinery.
**Recommended fix**: Promote `k_truth_lemma` into `Metalogic/Completeness.lean` as the single truth lemma; relocate the box-existence lemmas; delete `truth_lemma`/`d_truth_lemma` and the D-route box block; repoint all 15 `*_truth_lemma_applied`. → **Task 539**

### H2. Duplicated propositional-combinator wrapper layers (wrap/unwrap bridges) across three families
**Files**: `Temporal/Metalogic/PropositionalHelpers.lean` (wrap:51, unwrap:56 + 8 combinators), `Bimodal/Theorems/Perpetuity/Helpers.lean` (wrap:56, unwrap:60 + same family), `Bimodal/Theorems/Propositional/{Core,Connectives}.lean` (28 defs, ~58 `unwrap` uses)
**Description**: The same combinators (`doubleNegation`, `impTrans`, `lceImp`, `rceImp`, `dni`, `identity`, ...) are re-declared statement-for-statement in three places, each delegating to `Foundations/Logic/Theorems` generics through a local wrap/unwrap bridge that exists solely because `DerivationTree`-style derivability and the generic `InferenceSystem` derivability share no definitional surface. Modal additionally reproves combinators locally (e.g. private `imp_trans0`, `Modal/Metalogic/Intuitionistic/CanonicalModel.lean:223`) instead of consuming the generic layer at all — `Foundations/Logic/Theorems` is effectively consumed only by Bimodal.
**Impact**: The archetypal "needless bridge lemma" pattern the review targets; every new family re-emits the same wrappers.
**Recommended fix**: Prove the wrapper family once, generic over a Hilbert/`InferenceSystem` typeclass instance (as `GenericMCS` already does for the deduction theorem); retire the wrap/unwrap layers; also drop the 34 per-target `rfl` embedding-commutation restatements (`toModal_atom/bot/imp` etc.) subsumed by the generic `embed_*` simp lemmas (`Propositional/Embedding.lean:92-104`). → **Task 540**

### H3. LTL is a semantically unverified island
**File**: `Cslib/Logics/LTL/Embedding.lean:48`
**Description**: `Formula.toTemporal` is a syntax-only map; the file has zero theorems, yet its docstring asserts the translation "preserves semantics" (reflexive-vs-strict until reconciliation asserted, never proven). No file imports the module. LTL's proven results (GNBA correctness `gnba_language_eq`, ω-regularity, `ltlModelChecking`) cannot transfer to the Temporal tree.

**Recommended fix**: Prove `LTL.Satisfies φ v ↔ Temporal.Satisfies φ.toTemporal (natFlow v)` and wire at least one result across; or correct the translation if unprovable as stated. → **Task 541**
