# Phase 2a Handoff — subsOne Subformula-Complexity Lemma

## Status: COMPLETED

## What was proved (all in Normalization.lean, all axiom-free)
- `subs_maximalFormulas_mem` (membership characterization, by induction on input `body`):
  for `k ∈ (subs Ds body).maximalFormulas`, one of:
    1. `k ∈ body.maximalFormulas`, or
    2. `∃ A' (h : A' ∈ Γ'), k ∈ (Ds A' h).maximalFormulas`, or
    3. `∃ A' ∈ Γ', k = A'.complexity`  (new redex from substituting an intro at a former `ass A'`).
- `subsOne_new_redex_complexity_lt` (the Phase 2b interface):
    `k ∈ (D.subsOne E).maximalFormulas → k ∉ D.maximalFormulas →`
    `k ∈ E.maximalFormulas ∨ k = A.complexity`
  where `A` is the substituted hypothesis (type of `E`).

## IMPORTANT for Phase 2b
The lemma does NOT directly give `k < complexity(cut)`. It gives the disjunction
`k ∈ E.maximalFormulas ∨ k = A.complexity`. Phase 2b must:
  - discharge `k ∈ E.maximalFormulas` via the SN invariant (SN ⇒ `E.maximalFormulas = ∅`,
    using `sn_redexWeight_zero`/`maximalFormulas`-emptiness on strongly-normal subterms);
  - for the redex `impE (impI _ D) E`, the removed cut formula is `A → B`; the new redex
    complexity `A.complexity < (A → B).complexity` (proper subformula). Likewise for
    `orE (orI1/orI2 _ D) DA DB`: substituted hyp is the disjunct, a proper subformula of `A ∨ B`.
  - construct the Dershowitz–Manna witness: remove `{complexity(cut)}`, add only `A.complexity`
    (and nothing from E under SN), all `< complexity(cut)` ⇒ `Prod.Lex.left`.
