### H2. The single Modal sorry rests on a citation that does not contain the proof

**File**: `Cslib/Logics/Modal/Tableau/FrameSoundness.lean:1276` (docstring at 1238-1251)

`branchSatisfiableIn_s4FC_ancestor_redirect` is the only Modal sorry. Its docstring records
that four successive soundness routes appealed to Massacci (2000), *Single Step Tableaux for
Modal Logics*, Theorem 8.1 (blocking preserves satisfiability) — and that **in that paper
Theorem 8.1 is stated and never proved**. Appendix B.2 ("PROOFS OF SECTION 8") proves Theorem
8.4 only, deferring the π-modal-completed extension to references [7] and [20]. The task record
(582) and the in-file docstring agree on this attribution; it has been independently verified.

**Impact**: This is not transcription debt that more effort will close. The obstruction is that
the argument is absent from the source, so the work is genuinely *importing the model-graph
construction from the deferred references*, which is a research-scale obligation, not a proof
repair. Any milestone plan that budgets this as "one sorry" will be wrong.

**Recommended fix**: Treat 553 (can the guard be narrowed at all?) as a genuine decision point
with a real possibility of a negative answer. 582's own description already predicts the keyed
S4 soundness theorem is "likely FALSE" as stated. Budget for the outcome where the guard is
narrowed or the lemma restated, not proved.
